What exactly is AlertoQR?

AlertoQR is an enterprise-grade vehicle communication system masked as a sleek windshield decal.

Instead of writing your number on a piece of paper, you stick our high-quality QR tag on your car. If someone needs you to move your car, or notices you left your lights on, they simply scan it with their smartphone camera.

Our secure cloud servers instantly connect them to your phone without ever revealing your actual 10-digit mobile number.
