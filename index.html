What is AlertoQR?

AlertoQR is an enterprise-grade vehicle communication system engineered as a sleek windshield decal. We eliminate the risk of sharing your personal contact details on your dashboard. When your vehicle requires your attention, anyone can securely reach you through our encrypted cloud servers—keeping you instantly informed while maintaining total anonymity.
