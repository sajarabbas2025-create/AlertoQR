What exactly is AlertoQR?

AlertoQR is a highly secure, premium QR code sticker that goes on your vehicle's windshield.

If your car is parked wrong, about to be towed, or involved in an emergency, anyone can simply scan the QR code using their smartphone camera. A secure webpage opens, allowing them to Call or WhatsApp you instantly.

Your actual phone number is NEVER shown to the scanner.
