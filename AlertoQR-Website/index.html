What is AlertoQR?

Writing your phone number on your dashboard is a massive privacy risk. AlertoQR replaces that outdated method with a highly secure, smart QR code decal.


When scanned, it opens a secure, premium interface allowing the scanner to call or WhatsApp you instantly. Whether your headlights are left on, your car is blocking a driveway, or there is an emergency, you stay connected while your identity stays protected.
